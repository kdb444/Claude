How to Transfer: 5 Steps

1

Research

Find schools and programs that fit your goals.

1 · Research

Start a year before you plan to move. Compare programs, costs, and transfer-friendliness.

  • Shortlist 3–5 schools
  • Check each school's transfer admission page
  • Note deadlines and GPA requirements
2

Meet an Advisor

Get a plan from someone who knows the system.

2 · Meet an Advisor

Your current school's advisor and the destination school's transfer advisor can map out your path.

  • Ask about transfer agreements
  • Confirm which courses count
  • Build a semester-by-semester plan
3

Prerequisites

Finish the required courses and keep your GPA up.

3 · Prerequisites

Complete the courses your target major requires before you apply.

  • Major-specific prerequisites
  • General education requirements
  • Minimum GPA for admission
4

Apply

Submit applications, transcripts, and financial aid forms.

4 · Apply

Gather documents early; deadlines are firm.

  • Application and fee (or waiver)
  • Official transcripts from every school attended
  • Essays and recommendations if required
  • FAFSA / financial aid
5

Enroll

Get credits evaluated, register, and settle in.

5 · Enroll

Accept your offer and confirm how your credits transfer.

  • Pay deposit and accept admission
  • Review your credit evaluation
  • Attend orientation and register for classes
